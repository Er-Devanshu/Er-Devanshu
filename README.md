## 01 / The Person Behind the Profile

I build at the intersection of **Data, Engineering, and Artificial Intelligence**.

Currently working as a **Senior Tech Lead – Data & AI at Aegis Soft Tech**, I lead technical strategy, architecture, and engineering initiatives across Business Intelligence, Data Engineering, Machine Learning, and Generative AI.

With around 8 years of industry experience and an M.Tech in Data Science and Engineering from **BITS Pilani**, I bring together hands-on engineering, architectural thinking, and technical leadership.

I am equally passionate about the technology ecosystem outside the workplace. I lead six technology user groups, contribute to knowledge-sharing initiatives, mentor professionals, and speak at technical events.
