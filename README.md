## 👨‍💻 Behind the Code

**Data is where I started. Engineering is how I think. AI is what I'm exploring next.**

I'm **Devanshu Tayal**, a technology professional who enjoys solving complex problems, building things that scale, and bringing people together around technology.

With around **8 years of industry experience** and an **M.Tech in Data Science & Engineering from BITS Pilani**, I've worked across Data Engineering, Business Intelligence, Data Science and Artificial Intelligence.

Currently, I'm **Senior Tech Lead – Data & AI at Aegis Soft Tech Pvt. Ltd.**, leading technology strategy, solution architecture and engineering initiatives across the Data and AI ecosystem.

Beyond engineering, I build technology communities, speak at events, conduct workshops and teach at BITS Pilani.
