## 👨‍💻 Behind the Code

**Data is where I started. Engineering is how I think. AI is what I'm exploring next.**

I'm **Devanshu Tayal** — a technology professional who enjoys solving complex problems, building things that scale, and bringing people together around technology.

Over the last **8 years**, I've worked across the evolving landscape of Data Engineering, Business Intelligence, Data Science and Artificial Intelligence. From designing enterprise data platforms and analytics solutions to exploring LLM-powered applications and agentic systems, I enjoy connecting technology with problems that actually matter.

Today, as **Senior Tech Lead – Data & AI at Aegis Soft Tech Pvt. Ltd.**, I work on technology strategy, solution architecture and engineering across the Data and AI ecosystem.

But my journey isn't limited to what happens behind a screen.

I believe technology grows stronger when knowledge is shared. That's why I spend time building technology communities, speaking at events, conducting workshops and teaching at **BITS Pilani**.
